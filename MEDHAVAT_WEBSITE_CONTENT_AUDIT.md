## 6. Content Gaps & Redesign Opportunities

1. **Client Proof & Case Studies:**
   * While "10+ Projects Delivered" is prominently advertised, the live homepage lacks named client logos, published case study narratives, or verbatim client testimonials.
2. **Founder / Leadership Presence:**
   * Adding founder bios, leadership photos, and technical team credentials will significantly elevate conversion credibility for enterprise clients.
3. **Physical Address / Office Presence:**
   * Adding physical office location details and Google Maps integration will reinforce authenticity.
4. **Legal Compliance Pages:**
   * Missing `Privacy Policy`, `Terms of Service`, and `Cookie Policy` links in the footer.
5. **Interactive Engagement Elements:**
   * The contact form can be supplemented with direct WhatsApp chat triggers, calendar booking (Calendly/Cal.com), or an interactive project cost estimator.
